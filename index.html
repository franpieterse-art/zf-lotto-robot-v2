Data and fairness

This version contains a small dated archive collected from published Lotto results. It does not yet fetch arbitrary historical dates online. A date without verified results will show an error rather than invented numbers.

Predictions use only records dated before the chosen draw. Winning results stay hidden until Reveal is clicked. Historical patterns cannot reliably predict independent random draws.
