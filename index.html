Historical results data source and configuration

The robot needs verified historical results for each game. Main Lotto, Lotto Plus 1 and Lotto Plus 2 are kept separate. For now, upload a verified CSV or place draws.csv in GitHub. V12 first attempts the Lottology combined historical archive for all three games, then BetTip as a fallback, caches it for six hours, and reports connection or parsing errors. BetTip availability has not been independently verified. This app never invents draw results.
